Purpose and scope

LangGraph stock/crypto research and debate system producing typed ratings, resumable reports and outcome-based memory; decision-quality sweeps rather than portfolio fill simulation.

Design decisions

Decision Rationale Tradeoff Evidence
Join concurrent reports before debate. Graph waits for all first-step reports (and optional memory) before researcher rounds. Slowest analyst gates debate, and configured tool round bounds affect completeness. GraphSetup (L93)
Separate decision evaluation from portfolio simulation. Each rating claim can be scored without inventing unprovided fills/positions. Results are indicative: stochastic sampling and unarchived text feeds prevent exact replay. run_backtest (L129)

Strengths and limits

Strengths

  • Shape-specific per-ticker checkpoints and idempotent decision memory support resumable analysis. get_checkpointer (L42) / TradingMemoryLog (L11)

Limitations

  • No broker execution path or portfolio ledger; risk language and BUY/SELL ratings must not be presented as filled trades. run_backtest (L129) / PortfolioContext (L29) / create_portfolio_manager (L23)
